# Code of Conduct

This project is intended to be a welcoming place for technical collaboration.
Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent, and make
room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and publication of
someone else's private media or location information are not acceptable.
Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when needed to protect the community.

For a private safety or conduct concern, follow the private-reporting route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not place sensitive details in a public issue.
